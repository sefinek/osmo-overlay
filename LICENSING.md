## Permitted purposes

The licence permits any noncommercial purpose. It also expressly permits personal
research, experiments and testing for public knowledge, study, private entertainment,
hobby projects, amateur pursuits and religious observance without an anticipated
commercial application.

The **Noncommercial Organizations** section permits use by charitable organizations,
educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health
organizations, environmental protection organizations and government institutions,
regardless of their funding or obligations resulting from that funding. The licence
also preserves applicable fair use rights.

Examples of ordinary noncommercial use include adding telemetry to a personal travel
video or sharing a hobby project without a commercial purpose.

## Uses requiring separate permission

Unless a purpose is already permitted by the licence, obtain separate permission
before using Telemavix Studio for commercial purposes. Examples include:

- Creating videos to earn advertising revenue or to sell them.
- Creating sponsored content or promotional videos for a business.
- Providing paid rendering or editing services to clients.
- Other use of the software for a commercial purpose.

Contact [contact@sefinek.net](mailto:contact@sefinek.net) to discuss separate terms.
These examples explain the existing licence; they do not override its permitted
purposes or impose additional restrictions.

The purpose of using the software matters. Disabling monetization does not make a
business advertisement noncommercial. Conversely, a channel's participation in a
partner programme alone does not determine the purpose of every use of Telemavix Studio.
Consider the intended use and expected commercial benefit of the particular project.

## Rights to videos and other materials

The software licence governs use, modification and distribution of Telemavix Studio. It
does not place your original footage or music under PolyForm, transfer ownership of
your materials, or grant rights to third-party content. You must have the rights
needed for the recording, music, logos, maps and other materials you include.

A map provider's permission for commercial use concerns that provider's content or
service. It does not authorize commercial use of Telemavix Studio beyond the permissions
in the software licence.

## Maps and attribution

Check the terms of each map source and the service plan you use. Permission to use a
service commercially does not by itself establish permission to publish its maps in
a video. An unspecified use is presented as unknown, rather than as permission or
a prohibition.

Some map images, including those from [Thunderforest](https://www.thunderforest.com/terms/)
and [OpenTopoMap](https://www.opentopomap.org/about), use CC BY-SA. The applicable
licence requires attribution and share-alike for adaptations. Follow the licence's
requirements for identifying the licence and changes, too. Whether a particular video
is an adaptation or a collection depends on how the map is incorporated; the app
does not determine that for you. See the [CC BY-SA 4.0 overview](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
for Thunderforest and the licence identified by OpenTopoMap for its maps.

Keep required map credits when editing an export in another application, including
when compositing a green screen or alpha export. Other provider conditions continue
to apply. Recheck the terms if you change the map source, service plan or materials.

The app's acceptance button records acceptance of the software licence. It is not
proof that a map provider has authorized a particular use, and does not replace
registration or acceptance required by that provider. The map summary is informational
and does not certify that an export meets every applicable condition.

## Sharing the software

When distributing Telemavix Studio or permitted modifications, follow the licence's
distribution and notice requirements. Recipients must receive the licence text or
its specified URL, together with the applicable `Required Notice:` lines. Third-party
libraries and tools retain their own licences and notices.
